Tufts

🏆 Clear Winner for Bio

Interdisciplinary dept where biochemists work alongside marine ecologists. Active Rotjan Lab in marine ecology welcomes sophomores. BS/MS in 5 years for top students. Biotech co-major available. Affiliated med school pipeline is unmatched.

Boston College

⚠️ The Known Weakness

BC's science programs are widely acknowledged as underperforming relative to its overall ranking. The school excels in business, nursing, and humanities. Premed students succeed through drive, not institutional science infrastructure.

Santa Clara

✅ Solid but Limited

Good hands-on biology program with close faculty relationships. Strong emphasis on lab and field work. No affiliated med school, no marine science pathway, no named research labs with active undergrad slots. Good for what it is.
